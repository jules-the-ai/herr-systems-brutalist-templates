PLUG INTO THE WORK

Help that fits your delivery process.

A busy delivery period does not always require a permanent hire or a new way of working. Contract support can cover a defined feature, a group of page templates, a maintenance backlog, or a short stretch of hands-on implementation. The useful starting point is an agreed outcome, not a vague promise of unlimited availability.

We work within the repository, issue tracking, and review practices your team already uses where practical. Before work begins, we clarify access, decision-making, communication, and acceptance. That includes who can approve a change, who owns deployment, and what needs escalation instead of a silent workaround.

Capacity is agreed for the engagement; this page is not a claim of immediate staffing availability. We make dependencies visible, keep changes reviewable, and document the result. A good contract handoff leaves the team with usable work and context, not a feature that only the contractor understands.

01 // FROM QUESTION TO HANDOFF

A clear path through the work.

  1. 01

    Agree the engagement

    Confirm scope or capacity, availability, access, review ownership, confidentiality needs, and the intended handoff.

  2. 02

    Join the workflow

    Work in reviewable increments using agreed tickets, branches, and checkpoints. Raise blockers and changes to assumptions early.

  3. 03

    Close the loop

    Walk through completed work, record open items, transfer relevant documentation, and confirm which temporary permissions should be removed.

02 // GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

Can you work under our agency process?

That is the aim. We review the required tools, coding standards, communication rhythm, and approval process before agreeing to the engagement.

Is this unlimited on-demand support?

No. Availability, hours, response expectations, and deliverables are agreed in advance. A new urgent request may require reprioritization or a separate agreement.
