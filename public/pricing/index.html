A starting point.
Not a surprise invoice.

Hourly work is $70/hour. The calculator uses sample fixed-scope prices to help you plan. For a well-defined project, an agreed per-project price can be lower than the equivalent hourly total—not a guaranteed percentage discount.

We confirm the details first. Complexity, content readiness, existing systems, and the number of workflows affect the final quote. We’ll agree on inclusions, revision rounds, schedule, and change requests before starting.

Keep outside costs visible. Domain names, paid themes or plugins, third-party subscriptions, AI usage, payment processing, taxes where applicable, and work outside the agreed scope are not included in these samples. Monthly support is separate.
