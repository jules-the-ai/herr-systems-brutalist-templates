AUTOMATE THE BORING

Build a helper you can supervise.

An assistant is most useful when its job is specific. Sorting incoming requests, gathering research, preparing a draft, or summarizing a shared document can remove friction without handing over a whole business process. We look for a bounded task with a clear input, a useful output, and a person who can decide whether the result is right.

The setup includes the less exciting questions: what information the assistant can access, which tools it can use, where data travels, and when it must stop for approval. Reading a document is different from sending a message, editing a record, or spending money. Those permissions should never be treated as interchangeable.

AI output can be wrong, incomplete, or manipulated by material it reads. We test representative tasks and failure cases, make review points visible, and provide a way to pause the workflow. The goal is a practical tool with documented limits, not a claim that every answer will be accurate.

IS THIS WHAT YOU NEED?

A good fit if…

  • Your team repeats a research, sorting, or drafting task.
  • A human can review the proposed output before it affects customers.
  • You want to understand permissions and costs before connecting tools.

WHAT WE AGREE UP FRONT

A useful starting scope.

These are typical inclusions to discuss, not a fixed package. Deliverables, exclusions, timing, and ongoing costs are confirmed in writing.

  • A task definition and list of prohibited actions
  • Scoped tool access and credential-handling guidance
  • Explicit approval points for external side effects
  • Representative test tasks and failure examples
  • Run, pause, review, and handoff instructions

01 // FROM QUESTION TO HANDOFF

A clear path through the work.

  1. 01

    Choose one bounded job

    Map the task, review sample inputs, identify sensitive data, and agree on what the assistant may and may not do.

  2. 02

    Configure and challenge

    Connect only required tools, add approval gates, and test normal tasks alongside misleading inputs, missing context, and tool failures.

  3. 03

    Pilot with a person

    Run a small supervised trial, review outputs and costs, refine the workflow, and document how to pause, change, or remove access.

02 // GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

Can an assistant send emails or update records?

Only when that capability is explicitly included and authorized. Draft-only or read-only is the safer starting point. External actions need clear permissions, review rules, and a way to stop them.

Where does our information go?

That depends on the selected models, tools, and hosting. We map those services and their relevant data settings before connecting sensitive information; this sample page makes no blanket privacy guarantee.
