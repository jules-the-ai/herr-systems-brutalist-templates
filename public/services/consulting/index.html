THINK BEFORE YOU BUILD

Make the decision less mysterious.

A technical question is often a business question in disguise. Should you replace the website or improve it? Buy a tool or build one? Introduce automation now or fix the underlying process first? We turn a broad concern into a specific decision, then gather enough context to make the options comparable.

A useful review does not begin by assuming you need a rebuild. We look at the current setup, the people who maintain it, the time available, and the outcome you want. Existing tools may be good enough with a smaller change. If specialist advice is needed beyond the agreed review, we say so rather than presenting uncertainty as certainty.

The handoff is a prioritized recommendation with reasoning, dependencies, and next steps. You can use it with your own team or another provider. Implementation is a separate decision; a consulting engagement does not quietly commit you to a development project.

IS THIS WHAT YOU NEED?

A good fit if…

  • You need a second opinion before approving a proposal.
  • Your team has options but no shared set of priorities.

A clear path through the work.

  1. 01

    Frame the decision

    Agree on the question, the stakeholders, the materials needed, and what the review will not cover.

  2. 02

    Review and compare

    Inspect the agreed evidence, ask targeted questions, and compare practical options against cost, maintenance, risk, and team capacity.

  3. 03

    Prioritize the next steps

    Walk through recommendations, capture unresolved questions, and deliver a written plan with owners and dependencies where known.

02 // GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

Is consulting the same as a formal security audit?

No. A general technical review is not a penetration test, compliance certification, legal opinion, or exhaustive security audit. Specialized assessments require their own qualified scope.

Do we have to hire you for implementation?

No. The recommendations should be useful to your team or another provider. Any follow-on delivery work is separately scoped and agreed.
